About us.

Evelynx is a full-fledged creative and marketing agency built for businesses that intend to grow. We combine sharp creative thinking, market-savvy campaign strategy, and the technology to back it all up — one young, ambitious studio delivering what traditionally takes three separate agencies.

View Services →

We're a startup — deliberately, proudly, and structurally. That means every client works directly with the people doing the work. Ideas move fast, decisions happen in hours instead of committee weeks, and the energy of building something from the ground up flows into every brand we touch. Being new isn't our weakness; it's the reason we try harder, answer faster, and care more visibly than agencies that have grown comfortable.

Our strength is the blend. Most agencies pick a lane — creative, or marketing, or technology. We trained in all three because Nepali businesses shouldn't have to hire a designer who doesn't understand marketing, or a marketer who can't read a analytics dashboard. Creativity gives our work its spark; marketing discipline gives it direction; technology gives it reach. The combination is the product.

We understand that success in any market requires a deep understanding of our clients' businesses — their customers, their competition, and the gap between them. That's why every engagement starts with listening, not pitching. We take a tailored approach with each client, working closely to build customized strategies designed to deliver real results, then measuring everything so "results" is a number, not a feeling.

Our focus on data-driven, results-oriented work means our clients are equipped to compete and succeed in today's dynamic digital landscape — with creative that people remember and marketing that finance can verify.

We are passionate about what we do, and we take pride in helping our clients succeed.

More thanglobal —local first.

We are more than global, we are local — because creativity and marketing both land differently depending on where they're from. Festivals, humour, trust networks, how Nepali customers actually decide: this context is where our ideas are born. By staying close to the culture and community, we understand what inspires and motivates people here — which is what allows our campaigns and brands to resonate instead of feeling imported.

Local insight doesn't mean local standards. Everything we create is engineered to compete well beyond Kathmandu — designed, measured, and built to global benchmarks, so the brand that wins at home is ready for the world.

Our values.

Six commitments that define how we work — with clients and with each other. They're the foundation of every partnership we take on, and we hold ourselves to them in everything we deliver.

Creativity

Creativity is what sets us apart — the instinct to try the version nobody expected, in a campaign, a logo, or a line of code.

Innovation

We challenge the status quo, embrace change, and take calculated risks to deliver cutting-edge solutions.

Collaboration

Clients in the room, not on a stakeholder list. We build a collaborative environment where ideas are shared and creativity is nurtured — every step of the way.

Transparency

Honest scopes, honest invoices, honest post-mortems. Transparency fosters accountability, encourages feedback, and leads to better decisions — for us and for our clients.

Excellence

Paying attention to the smallest details and continuously pushing ourselves to improve. Details are the product — we ship work we'd put our own name on.

Human-to-Human

The power of human connection. Behind every brand and every screen is a person with a problem worth solving — we build for them, and we talk to them that way.
