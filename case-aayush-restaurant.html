Case Study — Aayush Restaurant

From a good kitchen
to a known brand.

Brand Identity Logo & Visual System Hospitality
Client

Aayush Restaurant, Kathmandu

Services

Brand Identity · Logo Design · Menu Design · Brand Guidelines

Year

2025

The brief.

Aayush Restaurant is a neighbourhood favourite in Kathmandu — the kind of place regulars recommend by name. But its brand hadn't kept up with its reputation: the menu, the signboard, and the socials each looked like they came from a different business, and nothing on the street made a passer-by stop. The owner's brief to us was simple — make the brand look the way the food tastes.

What we did.

We started in the dining room, not in Figma. We ate the menu, watched the service, and sat down with the owner to ask the questions that shape every identity decision: who actually eats here, what do they order twice, and what should a first-time guest feel in the first ten seconds? The answers became the foundation — an identity built on warmth, appetite, and pride of ownership rather than trends.

From that positioning, we delivered a complete identity system:

  • Logo & wordmark — a primary lockup with presence on a signboard, plus a compact monogram for stamps, packaging, and small screens
  • Colour palette — a warm, appetite-led core with supporting neutrals, tested to survive print, signage, and phone screens without drifting
  • Typography — a display face with character for headlines and a clean workhorse for everything the kitchen prints daily
  • Menu design — rebuilt as the brand's most-handled touchpoint: scannable, legible in low light, and unmistakably Aayush
  • Signage & collateral — signboard, takeaway packaging, and in-store applications applied consistently for the first time
  • Social media templates — so daily posts stay on-brand without a designer in the loop
  • Brand guidelines — a compact document covering logo usage, colour codes, clear space, and the do's and don'ts

The outcome.

Aayush Restaurant now runs on a single visual system. The signboard, the menu, the takeaway box, and the Instagram grid all come from the same rules — handed over as a guidelines document that any printer or social media manager can follow without us in the room. For a young studio, this is the kind of project we take the most pride in: a real local business that finally looks the way its food deserves.
