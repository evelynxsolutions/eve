What's included.

We buy hardware on your behalf, not a vendor's. Sourcing is vendor-agnostic — you get the specification that fits your need and budget, with the lifecycle plan (what it costs to run, when it gets replaced) written down.

Delivery doesn't end at the door: our engineers install and configure servers, networking, and endpoint devices on-site, and hand over documentation your team can actually use.

Good fit if…

You're setting up or upgrading an office and want equipment chosen on merit — not on whichever reseller called first.
