What's included.

One codebase, both app stores. We build with React Native — and Flutter where the project calls for it — so your iOS and Android apps ship together, behave identically, and stay cheaper to maintain than two native teams.

From first prototype to store submission and post-launch updates, we handle the full cycle — including the device-testing grind that separates an app that demos well from one that lives well.

You have one product idea but don't want to pay for — and juggle — two separate native development teams.
